Definitions and sources

GVA and employment shares use their respective totals for ISIC sections A–T. BERD shares use reported industry R&D expenditure, including any unallocated activities. Assigned BERD is the amount that can be attributed to a group from published data; it may cover only part of that group. BERD/GVA and per-worker indicators require complete, same-year inputs. Manufacturing covers groups 03–07; services covers groups 10–16.
