Business R&D by sector

Compare business R&D with value added, employment and goods exports across six broad sectors. Compiled by Handy Satria.

Industry R&D refers to business enterprise expenditure on R&D (BERD), classified by the performer's principal activity. Basque observations include companies and private non-profit institutions (PNPI); Singapore's RIE survey covers science and technology R&D. Gross domestic expenditure on R&D (GERD) is reported separately in the context table. Hatching indicates partial sector coverage. Unallocated amounts are shown separately.

Chart data

Assigned R&D is the expenditure that can be allocated from published observations and may represent only part of an industry group. R&D/GVA and R&D per employed person require complete group coverage. Denominators cover the entire sector, including non-business institutions. Employment includes all employed persons, not only researchers.

Methods and coverage

MHT: manufacturing 20, 21, 26–30; LMT: 10–19, 22–25, 31–33; KIS: J/K/M/P; LIS: G/H/I/L/N/O/Q/R/S/T; utilities: D/E; other production: A/B/F. Specialist R&D companies (M72) belong to KIS, irrespective of which industry benefits from the research.

Unallocated R&D includes unresolved cross-group aggregates, unavailable components, section U and source discrepancies. It is not “Other production”. Goods exports cover manufacturing products; LMT exports exclude division 33 services. Export shares use all goods exports as denominator. Nominal productivity uses basic-price GVA per person at market exchange rates, not PPP or hours worked.

Basque branches supply partial MHT, LMT, KIS and other-production amounts from 2003 through 2024. Native branch-label mappings do not establish complete ISIC class coverage. Mixed categories remain unallocated. Negative reconciliation remainders are retained in the audit and excluded from stacks and ratios.
