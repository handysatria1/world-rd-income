About the data and citation

Handy Satria (2026). Worldwide R&D and income explorer. Include the release and data build date when citing. BibTeX · Citation metadata · Archived releases.

National data: OECD, UNESCO UIS / World Bank WDI, Eustat and UK ONS. Industry and economy data: OECD RDS / TiVA / TiM / BTiGE, A*STAR, Eustat, UNIDO and China NBS. Downloads include source dates, units, flags and missing values. Industry comparisons are available for 6 broad sectors and 16 industry groups.
