Absorptive-capacity indicators: income-group means

Country values are arithmetic means of non-missing annual observations in the selected period. Each income-group value is the unweighted mean of its countries’ period means; the count and coverage are shown. Missing years are not filled. These descriptive averages do not identify causal absorptive-capacity thresholds.

Methods and coverage

WDI annual data · Eustat annual data · WDI requests and checksums · Eustat requests and checksums · Methods

Patent applications are filings at patent offices, not grants or counts of unique inventions. Journal articles refer to the WDI scientific and technical journal series, not all scholarly publications. The WGI government effectiveness estimate and the 0–100 absolute score are distinct series. Internet use follows the WDI series sourced from ITU. Bachelor's degree attainment is cumulative for the population aged 25 and over. Human Capital Index observations are not annual. Missing years are not imputed. The CSV records observation years, units, source records, country-code matching, flags and notes. Derived researcher totals are full-time-equivalent estimates, not headcounts.

R&D and innovation by income group
