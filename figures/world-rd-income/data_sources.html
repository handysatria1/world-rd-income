RDS dataflow agency is OECD.STI.STP; all three detailed queries cover 2000–2025. Units, CURRENCY, UNIT_MULT, price basis and base year are retained per observation. Constant PPP uses base year 2020 in the initial build; consult each row rather than assuming a universal base year. Field codes follow the archived FORD hierarchy. Combined groups overlap components and must not be added to them.

Estimated/provisional, confidentiality, break, definition and reconciliation flags remain available in the CSV/workbook. A missing, confidential or unreturned cell is not zero. The funding and research-type cubes do not jointly identify funding source × research type. OECD and national/UNESCO series can disseminate related underlying observations and are not independent measurements.

WDI's lastupdated is a dataset-level date, not the revision date of an individual observation.

4. Direct UNESCO Institute for Statistics archive

Provider: UNESCO Institute for Statistics (UIS). The downloader discovers the current Science → SDG 9.5 SCN-SDG.zip link on the official bulk-download page. Both that page and the exact archive are retained. The initial build uses release 202602 (February 2026 archive); the inventory records the actual release used by each build.

The parser selects EXPGDP.TOT (total R&D expenditure as a percentage of GDP) from SCN-SDG_DATA_NATIONAL.csv, with labels from SCN-SDG_LABEL.csv, metadata from SCN-SDG_METADATA.csv and the archive's release documentation. Observations are filtered to the supported 2000–2025 range.

Direct UIS provides a separate nominal-US$ alternative using same-year WDI total GDP; it is not an additional independent country-intensity provider in the scatter/history selection. UIS and WDI R&D series may repeat the same observations. MAGNITUDE is a categorical flag, not a multiplier: NIL is an observed zero, while non-applicable, suppressed or included-elsewhere observations remain missing as specified by the parser. Original metadata and flags are retained.

Attribution: UNESCO Institute for Statistics. The downloaded release supplies its data under CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO; derived UIS dollar amounts are adaptations using WDI GDP. Consult the exact archived release documentation for its terms and metadata.

The redesigned BERD survey starts in 2022. Older 2020–2021 research types are not backfilled as comparable observations. ONS research types are current costs, while capital is separate. The 2024 edition revises earlier business totals without supplying matching revised earlier type splits; the dashboard therefore retains each year's matched 2022/2023/2024 edition. UK research and performer panels can consequently have different totals/vintages. Original OECD business-research alternatives remain exported, rather than being mixed with ONS capital.

ONS funding/performance comparisons retain rounding allowances and disagreements. Higher-education 2024 estimates, UKRI/HEFB grouping and the intensity-vintage distinction are documented in the detailed UK/Basque source audit and per-row locators. No comparable UK government-performed 2024 research-type split was identified in the reviewed publications.

Other coverage review: INE

INE's 2024 regional R&D results, archived as ine_rd_regions_2024.html, were checked for a Basque research-type tabulation. They do not supply numerical inputs to the dashboard. The recorded gap concerns the public tables accessed; it does not rule out a custom tabulation from a statistical office.

7. What is reported, selected or derived?

  • Reported versus normalised: reported OECD/ONS millions and Eustat thousands are converted to units without inventing observations. Original value, scale, source file, flags and units remain in the annual databases; charts display expenditure in millions.
  • Country nominal US$ R&D: annual GERD/GDP (%) ÷ 100 × total GDP in current US$, matched to the same economy and year. This is derived, not reported nominal GERD. OECD, WDI and direct UIS alternatives are retained separately and must not be summed.
  • Basque intensity: same-year reported Eustat R&D in EUR ÷ Eustat total GDP in EUR × 100. Current US$: EUR ÷ Spanish WDI EUR/US$. Current PPP: EUR ÷ Spanish WDI EUR/international-dollar factor. Spain's national GDP PPP is a regional proxy, not a measured Basque or R&D-specific PPP.
  • Basque constant-price funding/research PPP: reported EUR × same-year OECD Spain total GERD at constant PPP ÷ OECD Spain total current-EUR GERD. This implied national proxy is conditional on rounded inputs and is distinct from the sector/field dataset's available price bases.
  • ONS research PPP: reported GBP × same-year OECD UK funding-cube total GERD in the selected PPP basis ÷ OECD UK total current-GBP GERD. Current and constant-2020 PPP alternatives are separate. This is not ONS's R&D deflator.
  • Provider selection: scatterplots choose the most R&D years in the selected window, preferring OECD on ties, with one provider per economy. Comparisons select across the union of windows and a common eligible sample. Histories select across the displayed range. Basque regional data use Eustat. Funding-dashboard intensity additionally considers the reviewed ONS series; it does not change scatter/history selection.
  • Averages and residuals: intensity is a mean of annual percentages. Breakdown bars use common available years across total and observed components. Composition is either a ratio of summed amounts or the explicitly selected mean of annual shares. Grey remainders and currency conversions are derived; missing research types stay missing. No missing factor or year is filled forward.

See the full methodology, the downloadable coverage/reconciliation tables and the individual chart CSVs for the exact selected years, source flags, conversions and remaining gaps. Data-provider observations may share an underlying source; multiple dissemination routes are not independent corroboration.
