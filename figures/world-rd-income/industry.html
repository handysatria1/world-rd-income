Units, combinations and missing values

OECD national-accounts and STAN amounts are scaled into national-currency units. TiVA publishes current USD millions, scaled here into USD units; no CNY conversion is inferred. STAN currency codes are unresolved; UNIDO catalog currency labels are not treated as verified historical currencies. UNIDO USD amounts are its published conversion. All amounts are current prices.

Combined activities are preserved in a separate download, never split into divisions. D, L, O, P and U are explicitly marked equivalents of single divisions. Blank values are missing. UNIDO numeric zeros are marked because its portal displays them as a dash. Country/year/activity/observation notes are in the metadata download.
